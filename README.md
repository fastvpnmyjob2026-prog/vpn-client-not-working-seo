## FAQ

**1) Why is my vpn client not working on public Wi‑Fi?**  
Captive portals and blocked VPN protocols are common. Sign into the Wi‑Fi portal first, then connect. If it still fails, switch servers/regions.

**2) The client says “Connected” but I have no internet.**  
That’s usually routing/DNS or a Kill Switch lock. Disconnect, disable Kill Switch temporarily, reconnect, then re-enable it after traffic flows.

**3) Why does it keep reconnecting or getting stuck on “Authenticating”?**  
Clock drift, bad credentials, or a stale tunnel profile can cause loops. Log out/in, then do a clean reinstall if the profile is corrupted.

**4) Does it support strong privacy defaults?**  
Yes: **AES-256 encryption**, **No-Logs policy**, and a **Kill Switch** are built in, with a **privacy and security focus**.
